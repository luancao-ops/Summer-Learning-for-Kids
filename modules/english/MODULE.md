## Topics

### Grade 3
- Vocabulary: greetings, numbers 1–100, colors, animals, family, school objects
- Phonics: alphabet sounds; short vowels (a, e, i, o, u)
- Reading: simple sentences (S + V + O)
- Grammar: I am / You are / He is; singular vs. plural (basic)

### Grade 4
- Vocabulary: food, sports, weather, transport, jobs, body parts
- Phonics: long vowels; consonant clusters (bl, br, cl, cr, dr, fl, fr)
- Reading: short paragraphs (3–5 sentences); main idea
- Grammar: present simple (do/does); have/has; can/can't; this/that/these/those

### Grade 5
- Vocabulary: environment, technology, travel, emotions, health
- Reading: short stories (2–3 paragraphs); identify characters and events
- Grammar: past simple (regular + irregular); future (will/going to); comparatives/superlatives
- Writing: short paragraph (topic + 3 details + conclusion)
